Title: An embedded band foliated by closed asymptotic lines and nonrigid smooth tight tori
Authors: Yuetong Zhang (Peking University)
Contact: gzhangyuetong@gmail.com; 2300010810@stu.pku.edu.cn
Primary category: math.DG
MSC (2020): 53A05; 53C24
Comments: 42 pages, 6 figures; manuscript ver507.

Abstract:
We show that smooth tight tori in $\mathbb R^3$ are not determined up to congruence by their intrinsic metrics. In fact, we construct a smooth metric on $T^2$ admitting a Cantor family of pairwise noncongruent tight isometric embeddings. These embeddings agree outside a fixed compact region of strictly negative curvature, and none contains an open planar subset.

The construction is based on an embedded negatively curved ruled band foliated by closed asymptotic curves. We show that, for a sufficiently thin principal-normal ruled band, nontrivial compactly supported infinitesimal bendings exist precisely when the return map of the nonruling asymptotic foliation is the identity. In this case, all such bendings are parametrized by smooth functions of one transverse variable. We realize this geometry inside a tight torus and use disjointly supported bendings to obtain the isometric embeddings through independent sign choices.
