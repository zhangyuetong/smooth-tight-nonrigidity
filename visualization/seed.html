What this step does

The spherical curve prescribes the moving normal frame. It does not itself become the torus center. The center is the spatial primitive Γr=aP\Gamma_r=aPΓr​=aP, with E=ζrE=\zeta_rE=ζr​ and P=−ζ×EP=-\zeta\times EP=−ζ×E. The corrugations supply visibility and embedding conditions. Closure and zero return are separate conditions and both must be enforced.
