What this step does

Move the circuit slider from zero to one. The fixed cross-section carries all starting points; the colored curves grow along the surface; the returned markers appear at the end. Each trajectory can move a long way in the ruling direction during the circuit. Identity holonomy only says that the final displacement on the section is zero.

In the turning view, the same broad patch is rigidly rotated into a local frame. The units are unchanged. Colored curves bend across straight rulings. Both families have zero normal curvature, even though the ambient curves themselves need not be straight. The curvature view colors the magnitude of negative Gaussian curvature, not its sign: all plotted KKK values are negative.

A surface crossing is a crossing of images of different parameter points. The return map lives on the annulus's parameter domain. Following a leaf through a crossing means continuing its differential equation on its original sheet; it does not switch to the other visible sheet.

Parameter overdo test · scope of this model

Three integration tolerances are compared against the exact full leaf formula and against the starting section. The curvature tests differentiate the computed physical positions in both raw parameter directions and reconstruct both fundamental forms; they do not merely reevaluate the displayed formula for KKK. Every tested curvature value is negative. Smaller finite-difference steps eventually suffer cancellation, so the table retains both tested steps. Return coordinates and curvature checks use Float64 data; the browser receives a Float32 drawing cache.
