What this step does

The outer annulus does not determine the difficult inner geometry. It closes the already completed saddle annulus using its exact parabolic boundary collars. Its radius is strictly larger than RNR_NRN​ between the two circles, while the saddle part projects inside RNR_NRN​, so the interiors are disjoint.

Tightness concerns the global distribution of normals and height-function critical points. A complicated local band is compatible with it because the negative part is still a single injective Gauss chart after completion.
