What this step does

The seam keeps its position and tangent plane. The perturbation changes how nearby asymptotic trajectories approach or leave it. One seam becomes repelling, the other attracting. Small positive boundary graphs inside those collars provide the data needed by the connector.

The gold protected interval lies between the collars and is untouched. This makes it possible to carry the bending into a completed surface without trying to extend the bending through the new pieces.
