What this step does

The extra-wide valid band uses α=0.85\alpha=0.85α=0.85, so its minimum denominator is still 0.150.150.15. Its widest ruling is about 4.114.114.11 units. This makes the widening limit concrete: the problem is a finite leaf running to infinity in its reciprocal formula, rather than a local failure of negative curvature.

Switch to the nonidentity control and run the slider. Its center is still closed, its speed is positive, and its raw ruled surface is still negatively curved.
