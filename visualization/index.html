What this step does

The gold patch in the torus is the same computed band that appears in the close views. Each attachment starts from its actual position, value and gradient. There is no replacement of a tangled surface by an independently chosen torus. The convex exterior alone is cut away in the first view.

The difficulty is keeping both the support source and its gradient globally one-to-one while extending the band. The connector controls one boundary; duality exchanges the two maps; the quadratic filling controls the next boundary. Two explicit end germs then make closure possible.
