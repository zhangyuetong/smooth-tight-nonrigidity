What this step does

The source segments are straight, but their physical images need not be. Switch between the physical, source and gradient views: they are three maps of the same connector. At the incoming edge the physical strip meets the actual band. At the outgoing edge its gradient trace is circular while its source trace can still be quite wavy.

That mismatch is deliberate. Ver500 needs only a circular gradient at this stage. Duality turns it into the circular source seam required by the next filling.
