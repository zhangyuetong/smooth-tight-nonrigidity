What this step does

The wide view adds actual material to the same ruled surface. Its colored curves remain the explicit closed asymptotic leaves. The microscope rotates and magnifies an actual small patch uniformly, so you can see its interior structure without changing the metric or twisting its transverse direction artificially.

A wider surrounding band can be useful for understanding holonomy. It cannot automatically be used for the completed torus: the source and gradient maps, both visibility conditions, and the exit calculations must be checked again.

The wide model is explicitly the unperturbed surrounding band, not the finished wide torus. The completed example retains the narrow exit levels.
