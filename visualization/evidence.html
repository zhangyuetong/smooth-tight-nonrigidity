What this step does

The numerical checks answer specific questions: does the return close, do the actual exit signs pass, are the connector boundary maps nested, do the quadratic signs hold, and do the explicit radial ends meet? They must not be bundled into an unsupported claim that the entire smooth construction has been certified.

This page gathers the results and distinguishes what is implemented from what still relies on the manuscript.

Mechanism and mathematical details

Implemented: the balanced seed; literal ruled band; paper's spherical-Fermi support perturbations; both visible connectors; both differential Legendre coordinate changes; quadratic fillings; smooth radial neck adapter; polar boundary circle; reflection; convex meridian; affine image; protected signed bending patches.

Remaining numerical implementation: the relative two-dimensional smoothing of the band/connector and connector/filling interfaces; exhaustive global embeddedness validation of the smoothed finite-NNN surface; the amplitude bound for an embedded tight displayed pair. The paper supplies analytic arguments in its stated regime. The current mesh is a piecewise same-data reconstruction with those limits.

The paper map lists every statement and section. Later applications, countable families and the general ruled-band classification are marked as manuscript-only extensions rather than silently omitted or presented as calculated scenes.
